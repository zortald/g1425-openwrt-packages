OpenWrt for Nokia G-1425-MA and G-1426-MA

OpenWrt snapshot (Linux 6.18) for two Nokia GPON ONTs. The stock firmware stays in the other flash bank as a fallback.

The two models are different hardware (SoC, CPU byte order, Wi-Fi, flash layout). Each has its own firmware — never flash one model's image onto the other.
